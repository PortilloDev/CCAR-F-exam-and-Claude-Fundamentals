**Lecture ID:** 58006613

## Transcript

A practice question can feel familiar even when its correct answer depends on a different constraint from one you've seen before. Recognising a topic is useful, but you also need to explain why a particular option addresses the stated problem.

### What this chapter covers

- **Question analysis:** identifying the goal, the hard constraint, and the observed failure in an unfamiliar scenario.
- **Exam objective → question:** how an exam domain becomes a specific question; distinguishing the actual requirement from surrounding detail.
- **Distractors:** incorrect choices that add unnecessary complexity, address the wrong cause, or answer a different question than the one asked.
- **Decision checklist:** per-task checklist to identify weak areas for further study.
- **Timed practice:** mixed practice set under timer — explaining why each rejected option fails the requirement.

**Goal:** Reason through unfamiliar questions, explain why each answer meets or fails the requirement, and use practice results to plan remaining preparation.
