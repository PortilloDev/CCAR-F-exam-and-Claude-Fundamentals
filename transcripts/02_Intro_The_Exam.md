## Transcript

Welcome to the course. Preparing for the Claude Certified Architect Foundations exam means learning to make design decisions about systems that use Claude. For example, a customer support assistant might propose a refund, but your application still needs to check the customer's identity before moving money. We'll use decisions like this to connect the exam topics with practical development work. You'll see how both the technology works and how to choose an approach that meets the stated requirements. We'll start with a typical task an architect needs to solve: deciding when a model's judgement is useful and when application code must enforce a fixed rule. We'll explain what deterministic enforcement means and work through a refund example, comparing stronger instructions, worked examples and a check in code. Then we'll review the exam objectives and explain how to prioritise your learning time using the domain weights and the areas you need to improve. We'll also explain the course structure, including lectures, quizzes, labs and preparation exams, and how to use each part during your studies. After this chapter you'll be able to justify where a rule should be enforced, identify your study priorities and plan how to work through the course.
