# Lecture 102: Intro: Deciding Under Uncertainty

**Section:** Context and Reliability  
**Lecture ID:** 58006611

---

## Transcript

When a research report contains conflicting figures, choosing one number can hide uncertainty the reader needs to understand. Incomplete sources, ambiguous customer information, or unverified records all require different responses.

### What this chapter covers

- **Escalation triggers:** policy gaps, requests for a person, lack of progress — why tone or a raw confidence score is insufficient.
- **Ask vs. escalate:** when to clarify identity (tool can find the answer), when to ask the customer (they have the answer), when to escalate (policy gap — only a human can decide).
- **Human review & calibration:** labeled examples to set confidence thresholds; stratified sampling to find document types that underperform.
- **Provenance & conflicting sources:** structured reports with source metadata; retaining both conflicting values instead of averaging or ignoring.
- **Meaningful research reports:** well-established findings, contested findings, coverage gaps — preserving table/list/prose format so meaning is not lost.

**Goal:** Choose an appropriate response to uncertainty and communicate what still needs checking.
