### How to register

1. **Check eligibility:** registration is currently limited to companies that are part of Anthropic's partner network. Verify your company is listed.
2. **Use your company email** to log in to the Anthropic partner network.
3. **Use the register link** — it redirects to Pearson VUE (the exam host).
4. **Select exam at Pearson VUE** — choose between in-person or remote.
5. **Book the appointment** — have personal ID ready (must match exam registration).

---

### In-person vs. remote

| | In-person | Remote |
|--|-----------|--------|
| Commute | Required | None |
| Desk scan | Not required | Required |
| Bio breaks | Allowed | Not allowed |
| Nerves | Typically calmer | Can be more stressful (setup, scan) |

**Recommendation:** if a Pearson VUE center is nearby, consider in-person — easier logistics and typically less stressful. Remote is also fine if well-prepared.
